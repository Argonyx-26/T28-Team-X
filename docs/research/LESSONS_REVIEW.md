## Dividing fractions · divided without flipping the second fraction · en

It looks like you forgot to flip the second fraction when dividing. No worries, this is a common step that many students miss!

**Rule:** To divide fractions, keep the first fraction, change the division sign to multiplication, and flip the second fraction (find its reciprocal).

**Example:** 3/5 ÷ 3/10
Keep 3/5, change ÷ to x, flip 3/10 to 10/3.
So, 3/5 x 10/3 = (3 x 10) / (5 x 3) = 30/15 = 2.

**Tip:** Remember "Keep, Change, Flip" (KCF)!

- 1/2 ÷ 1/4 → 2
- 2/3 ÷ 4/5 → 5/6
- 5/6 ÷ 1/3 → 5/2

- Which is bigger: 1/5 or 1/3? → 1/3

## Word problems · picked the wrong operation for the story · en

**Example:** If a recipe calls for 1/2 cup of flour and you want to make 3 batches, how much flour do you need?

**Correct Rule:** "Of" or "batches of" usually means multiply.

**Tip:** Read the problem carefully and look for words that suggest adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing!

**Rule:** To find an equivalent fraction, you must multiply (or divide) the **numerator** (top number) and the **denominator** (bottom number) by the **same non-zero number**.

**Example:** To find an equivalent fraction for 2/5, multiply both by 3:
2/5 = (2 x 3) / (5 x 3) = 6/15

**Tip:** Think of it like scaling up or down a recipe; you have to change all ingredients proportionally!
