# Evidence brief: what the data and published teacher surveys say

Researched on-site on 25 Sep 2026. Every item links to its source.

**Key:** **S** = statistic, **Q** = published quote (never our own interviews), **R** = research finding.

Before any item goes on a slide, Risheeth re-checks it against the source page.

## Three things that change the pitch
- **Karnataka coaching is 8.2%, not 27%.** MoSPI 2025 puts private coaching at 27% of students nationally but only 8.2% in Karnataka (9.7% in Classes 6–8). So lead with budget private schools (Karnataka has 19,105 private unaided schools), and put tuition centres second.
- **Indian trials show that diagnosis alone does not raise learning.** Neither the Andhra Pradesh diagnostic reports nor the CCE programme produced gains. GuruGraph is a diagnosis *plus* a lesson, retry questions and a teacher plan, and the pitch should say so.
- **The 29% is an average score, not a share of students.** It's the average percentage of fraction questions answered correctly. It is not the percentage of students who can do fractions.

## Sources
**S** statistic · **Q** published quote · **R** research finding. All items were checked on the source page unless marked *secondary*/*unverified*.

## 1. Learning levels
- Class 6 answered only **29%** of fraction items correctly (represent/compare ½, ¼), the lowest Grade 6 maths competency (operations/tables: 53%) · S · NCERT PARAKH Rashtriya Sarvekshan 2024 National Report (2025), p.7 · https://parakh.ncert.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-07/REPORT_India_IND.pdf
- Rural Std VIII: 45.8% can do a 3-digit ÷ 1-digit division · S · ASER 2024 · https://asercentre.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/ASER-2024-National-findings.pdf
- NAS 2021 Class 8 maths: 36% (Class 5: 44%) · S · *secondary* (Drishti IAS 2022; nas.gov.in unreachable) · https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-news-analysis/national-achievement-survey-nas-2021
- Delhi sample: the average Grade 6 student was 2.5 grade levels behind in maths, and one grade "spanned five to six grade levels" · R · J-PAL/Muralidharan et al. 2019 · https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/disrupting-education-evidence-technology-aided-instruction-india

## 2. Workload and class size
- 22–25% of teachers felt they had "too much correction work"; 44% of govt and 18% of private teachers felt they had too much administrative work · S (teacher-reported) · TISS SoTTTER 2023 (3,615 teachers, 8 states), pp.20–21 · https://tiss.ac.in/uploads/files/SOTTTER_23_final_version.pdf
- Multigrade classes: 38% of govt vs 19% of private schools (sample PTR 31:1 vs 21:1) · S · same
- Delhi time-use study (200 teachers): teaching took only 39–41% of school time; 66% called non-academic work their biggest hurdle · S · Accountability Initiative 2019 · https://accountabilityindia.in/blog/relooking-non-teaching-roles-of-government-school-teachers/
- Upper-primary PTR is 17 (India and Karnataka). Karnataka has 7,349 single-teacher schools (2.23 lakh students) · S · UDISE+ 2024-25, Tables 2.2/4.12 · https://dashboard.udiseplus.gov.in/report-new-v.6-demo2025/static/media/UDISE+2024_25_Booklet_existing.118ba29d4773e6372f72.pdf

## 4. Diagnosis and feedback evidence
- Feedback: +6 months of progress, "high impact for very low cost" (155 studies); low attainers benefit more · R · EEF Toolkit (2021 review) · https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/feedback
- Mindspark: +0.37 SD maths and +0.23 SD Hindi in 4.5 months (619 students, Delhi) · R · Muralidharan, Singh & Ganimian, AER 2019 · https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20171112
- TaRL: +0.15 SD (Haryana) and +0.70 SD (UP) in language · R · Banerjee et al., NBER 2016 · https://www.nber.org/papers/w22746
- **Design lesson:** in an Andhra Pradesh RCT, diagnostic feedback reports alone gave no learning gains · R · Muralidharan & Sundararaman 2010 · https://www.povertyactionlab.org/evaluation/impact-diagnostic-feedback-teachers-student-learning-india-0
- CCE had no effect "due to the program's focus on evaluation without linking evaluations to changes in teaching practices" · R · Berry et al., J. Dev. Econ. 2020 · https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/deveco/v143y2020ics0304387818315748.html

## 5. Fraction misconceptions
- US 6th/8th graders treated numerator and denominator as separate whole numbers (3/5+1/4=4/9) on 26% of addition problems (all operations: low achievers 32%, high achievers 10%) · R · Siegler & Pyke, Developmental Psychology 2013 · https://siegler.tc.columbia.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/2013-Siegler-Pyke-fac.pdf
- Low achievers were wrong on 46% of the answers they were most confident about, so students rarely catch their own errors · R · same
- How common "adding denominators" is among Indian students: **unverified** (no public figure).

## 8. Competitors / adjacent
- **Mindspark (Ei):** adaptive, misconception-based practice in Indian languages; needs a device per child and doesn't read notebooks · https://hundred.org/en/innovations/mindspark-vernacular-personalised-adaptive-learning
- **Khanmigo:** free for Indian teachers since Nov 2024 (English/Hindi); generates assessments and lesson prep, not diagnosis of student work · https://scoonews.com/news/khan-academy-launches-khanmigo-ai-tool-for-teachers-in-india/
- **Shiksha Copilot (Microsoft Research + Sikshana):** Kannada/English lesson plans for Karnataka govt teachers; planning only · Microsoft URL above
- **Gradescope (Turnitin):** groups and scores scanned handwritten answers against rubrics, only on fixed-template PDFs · https://guides.gradescope.com/hc/en-us/articles/24838908062093-AI-assisted-grading-and-answer-groups
- **Photomath (Google):** the student photographs a problem and gets the correct steps; there is no teacher view · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photomath

## Pitch-ready lines
1. "In India's 2024 national survey of 21 lakh children, Class 6 got only 29% of fraction questions right, their weakest maths skill." [PARAKH 2024, NCERT]
2. "Fewer than half of rural Class 8 students can do a simple division." [ASER 2024]
3. "In a TISS survey of 3,615 teachers, about one in four said they had too much correction work." [SoTTTER 2023]
4. "As one Karnataka teacher told Deccan Herald this month: 'Sometimes, we are being treated as clerks.'" [Deccan Herald, 4 Sep 2026]
5. "The mistakes are predictable. In a US study, students added tops and bottoms separately on a quarter of fraction-addition problems." [Siegler & Pyke 2013]
6. "Feedback is one of the cheapest high-impact tools in education, worth about six extra months of progress." [EEF Toolkit]
7. "Indian trials show diagnosis alone doesn't move scores, so every GuruGraph diagnosis comes with a lesson and retry questions." [Muralidharan & Sundararaman 2010; Berry et al. 2020]

## Do not claim
- "29% of Class 6 students can do fractions" (and "53% know tables"). These are average % correct on items, not shares of students.
- "Teachers spend only 19% of time teaching" (NIEPA 2018). It exists only in news reports.
- National tuition rates applied to Karnataka (8.2%). The tuition-centre market is thinner there.
- ASER as Bengaluru data. It is rural only.
- Black & Wiliam's (1998) "0.4–0.7 SD" for formative assessment as settled. Kingston & Nash 2011 found about 0.20 (https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ951173).
- Mindspark's +0.37 SD as a GuruGraph outcome.
- "NAS Class 8 maths 44%" (that is Class 5), or a single Grade 6 maths average (outlets say 46% or 50%).
